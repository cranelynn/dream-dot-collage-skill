# Example Cases: One Photo, One Output

These two cases are separate demonstrations. They must never be combined into one image.

## Case 1 — Dolomites mountain village

**Source photo:** a vertical travel photo showing jagged Dolomites peaks, an alpine village and church, green hillside, and trees.

**Candidate cutouts from this photo only:**
- Mountain ridge silhouette.
- Church and nearby village cluster.
- One small barn or fir-tree silhouette.

**Art direction:** muted sky-blue solid circle, warm-white page background, thin white die-cut borders, a few delicate orbit lines and stars, one tiny handwritten place label, and a minimal sleeping puppy near the lower edge.

**Reject if:** the complete original landscape is placed inside the circle, any element from the cathedral photo appears, or the cutouts still retain their original background.

## Case 2 — Gothic cathedral and visitor

**Source photo:** a vertical photo showing a visitor at the lower left and detailed Gothic cathedral architecture.

**Candidate cutouts from this photo only:**
- The visitor, carefully isolated from the surrounding architecture.
- A distinctive section of the ornate stone balustrade or arch.
- One architectural detail such as a carved pinnacle or statue, if it can be isolated cleanly.

**Art direction:** warm stone or pale slate-blue solid circle, warm-white page background, white die-cut borders, fine hand-drawn lines, a few small stars, and a minimal sleeping puppy. Preserve the person's appearance and avoid overloading the frame with architectural fragments.

**Reject if:** any mountain, village, sea, or other content from a different photo appears; the full cathedral photo is used as a circle fill; or the person is replaced with a generated lookalike.

## How to publish the visual demo

For each case, add a reviewed input/output pair with clear labels such as **Original** and **Dream Dot Collage**. Keep the two pairs separate. Do not describe a case plan as a completed visual demo until the output image has been generated, checked for genuine cutouts, and added to the repository.
