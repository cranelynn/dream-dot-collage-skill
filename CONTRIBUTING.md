# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving Dream Dot Collage.

## Keep changes aligned with the design
- Preserve the one-reference-image-per-output rule.
- Keep the dream circle flat and mostly empty.
- Prefer fewer, more meaningful source cutouts over additional decoration.
- Keep the puppy line art minimal and the composition spacious.
- Never add example images without permission from the people who own them.

## Before opening a change
- Keep the core workflow concise and actionable.
- Update the quality checklist when introducing a new rule or fixing a recurring failure mode.
- Verify that all links in `SKILL.md` and `README.md` point to existing files.
- Avoid claims of exact photo preservation unless the implementation actually preserves source pixels.
